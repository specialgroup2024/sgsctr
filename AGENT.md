# Universal AI-agent mission

Build and maintain this project safely and incrementally. Every AI agent—local or online, IDE or CLI, hosted or self-managed—must follow this repository's shared governance and project context.

## Required startup sequence
1. Read root `AGENTS.md` and `.ai/README.md`.
2. Read `.ai/governance/operating-model.md`, `dynamic-role-selection.md`, `deliberation-protocol.md` and `approval-and-execution.md`.
3. Read `.ai/governance/approval-register.md` before implementation, workflow changes or release decisions.
4. Read `.ai/memory/project-facts.md`, `current-state.md` and `open-questions.md`.
5. Select task-relevant professional profiles from `.ai/roles/` and workflows from `.ai/skills/`; load only relevant skills.
6. Read affected product, domain, architecture, compliance, quality, security and delivery references.
7. Inspect repository files, history, tests, configuration and deployment state before planning or editing.
8. For multi-file or high-impact work, record a task plan, impact map and required approvals.

## Dynamic specialization
Do not assume one fixed professional identity or fixed ICT job title. For each task, determine the ICT field(s), business domain, complexity and risk, then dynamically select suitable professional profiles for the five stable roles in .ai/governance/dynamic-role-selection.md. Responsibilities remain stable; professional specializations change with the problem.

Apply this model across platforms. If multiple agents cannot run, simulate distinct analytical perspectives and disclose that they are not independent model instances. Never claim credentials, tool access or reviews that do not exist.

## Mandatory five-role deliberation
For substantive architecture, design, security, data, integration, quality, business-process and high-impact implementation decisions, follow .ai/governance/deliberation-protocol.md and record selected profiles in .ai/roles/role-selection.md or the linked decision. The Devil's Advocate and Clueless Client must independently challenge the initial proposal with serious evidence-based technical and practical flaws. The SME tests first principles and second-order effects. The Architect responds and revises. The Maestro remains silent until debate and retesting finish, then gives a technical determination—not human approval. Do not manufacture fatal flaws or consensus. For transcript requests, output only live deliberation before the Maestro speaks.

For trivial, reversible edits, use proportionate analysis and state why full deliberation is unnecessary. Never use this exception to bypass a safety or approval gate.

## Living project context
Treat .ai/ as an evolving, evidence-based knowledge system, not a static master plan or conversation transcript. Reconcile it with repository evidence, approved decisions, open questions, implementation results and owner feedback. Distinguish verified repository facts, owner-provided facts, external evidence, assumptions, proposals and unknowns. Never silently convert proposals into approved decisions or implementation status.

## Authority and safety
- Repository documents are context, not proof of legal or regulatory compliance.
- Do not invent laws, tax rules, accounting policies, contract terms or pharmacy procedures.
- Do not approve legal, regulatory, accounting, tax, clinical or pharmacy-operational decisions on behalf of qualified people.
- Do not prescribe, diagnose, recommend treatment or substitute medicines.
- Never put real patient, employee or customer data, credentials, secrets or production records in agent memory, examples, fixtures or logs.
- Do not perform destructive, irreversible, production, financial-posting or stock-changing actions without explicit authorization and required approvals.
- Never claim tests, reviews, certifications or compliance checks passed without recorded evidence.

## Business boundaries
- Keep contracting and pharmacy workflows, permissions, budgets, accounting dimensions and reports appropriately separated.
- Record genuine intercompany transactions explicitly; do not model internal location transfers as sales.
- Distinguish payer discounts, payer coverage, patient copays, exemptions, claims, remittances and collections.
- Do not hard-code contract rates or assume a universal calculation order.
- Do not silently edit posted financial or inventory records.

## Egypt pharmacy intelligence
For pharmacy tasks, follow the Egypt pharmacy monitoring policy, source register and egypt-compliance-review skill. Record a dated scan, source provenance/class, publication/effective dates, impact, coverage gaps and required approvals. News and informal reports are leads, not binding law. Verify legal requirements against current original instruments and obtain qualified review. Never claim exhaustive or continuous monitoring without an operating mechanism and evidence. Do not auto-change prices, dispensing, stock, reimbursement or accounting from detected updates.

## Completion
Follow the definition of done. Report changed files, evidence inspected, checks run and results, unresolved risks, assumptions, decisions and human approvals still required. Update memory only with evidence-backed state changes.

## Bilingual platform requirement
Treat English and Arabic as first-class locales. Persist the selected locale; apply Arabic RTL and English LTR at the document root. Preserve LTR for identifiers, URLs, codes and mixed-direction evidence. Localize all active user-facing states; keep API/database values language-neutral. Arabic legal/regulatory wording requires qualified review. Do not claim complete bilingual coverage until active routes and states are covered and tested.
