## Professional, industry and market sources

Discover and maintain current official channels for the Egyptian Pharmacists Syndicate, Egyptian pharmaceutical manufacturers and distributors, chambers/trade associations, licensed wholesalers, major suppliers, healthcare providers, payer networks, public tenders, reputable Egyptian business/health media and relevant market research. Record each exact source URL, publisher, source class, coverage, reliability limitations and review date before relying on it.

Public social channels and crowdsourced availability reports may reveal early shortage, recall or market signals. Treat them as unverified leads until corroborated by EDA, the manufacturer/marketing authorization holder, licensed supplier or another competent primary source.

## International references

WHO, EMA, FDA and other foreign regulators may provide scientific or comparative context. They are not Egyptian law and do not replace EDA/MOHP or Egyptian legal instruments. Clearly label foreign-only information and assess whether EDA has adopted or referenced it.

## Source maintenance

- Revalidate each URL and the authority's remit at least quarterly and whenever a link fails.
- Add new Egyptian authorities, portals, trade bodies and credible market sources discovered during research; do not restrict searches to this initial list.
- Record inaccessible sources, login/paywall restrictions and missing archives as coverage gaps.
- Never scrape, bypass access controls or violate terms. Use permitted feeds/APIs or manual review.
- Keep source class, last checked, next review and owner in the evidence register.
