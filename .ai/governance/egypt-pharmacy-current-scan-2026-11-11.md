## Limitations and actions

- This is a bounded first scan, not a claim that all Egyptian sources or all updates were found.
- An index entry does not replace the original instrument. Verify full Arabic text, publication, commencement, amendments, transition provisions and applicability.
- No product-level registration, current official price, stock availability, recall action or company licence was checked in this scan.
- No legal, pharmacist, quality, tax or accounting approval is recorded.
- Next review: before any pharmacy requirements/design/release task, and during the weekly/monthly monitoring cycle in the monitoring policy.
