# Certification and assurance roadmap

This is an evaluation roadmap, not an approval to pursue certification and not a claim that the company or application is certified.

## Separate three concepts
- **Standards:** published requirements or guidance that can inform controls and tests.
- **Staff credentials:** qualifications held by named people and verified with the issuing body.
- **Organization certification:** independent assessment of a defined management-system scope by a competent certification body.

## Candidate management-system standards
| Candidate | Purpose from supplied source | Evaluation gate |
|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | Information-security management system. | Define legal entity, sites, services, assets, risk method, ISMS scope and independent certification route. |
| ISO/IEC 27701:2025 | Privacy information management. | Map controller/processor roles, personal and health-data flows, legal basis, cross-border transfers and privacy governance. |
| ISO/IEC 42001:2023 | AI management system. | Consider only if AI development/use is in scope; define AI inventory, risks, human oversight and governance. |

The editions above are reproduced from the supplied standards note dated 2026-11-07. Verify current editions, amendments, transition arrangements and official publication status before adoption or procurement.

## Staged evaluation
1. Business owner defines the goal, legal entity/entities, budget, accountable executive and intended certification claim.
2. Qualified advisers map legal obligations and determine whether a management-system standard adds value beyond mandatory Egyptian requirements.
3. Define scope, boundaries, interested parties, assets/data, risk method, policies, responsibilities, objectives and measurable outcomes.
4. Perform a documented gap assessment against the selected official standard; identify controls, owners, evidence and remediation.
5. Operate the management system long enough to generate real records: risk treatment, training, access reviews, incidents, supplier reviews, internal audits and management reviews as applicable.
6. Conduct readiness review and independent certification-body selection; verify accreditation, scope, competence, costs and audit cycle.
7. Obtain explicit owner authorization before engaging a certification body or making public claims. Preserve certificate number, scope, issuer, validity and surveillance status if achieved.

## Egypt-specific gate
An ISO certificate or OWASP test does not replace Egyptian legal, tax, privacy, pharmacy, licensing or professional obligations. Maintain the Egyptian requirements register and obtain qualified Egyptian legal, tax, accounting, regulatory-affairs, pharmacy and quality review where applicable.

## Prohibited claims
Do not describe SGSCTR, Special Group or any business unit as certified, compliant or audited unless independent evidence supports the exact entity, scope, standard, certificate and validity.
