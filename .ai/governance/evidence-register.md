# Evidence register

Status: living index for project and Egyptian pharmacy intelligence evidence.

## Evidence record

Each record must include:
- Evidence ID and title.
- Evidence type: repository, owner-provided, primary legal, official regulator, official market, professional/industry, media or informal signal.
- Publisher/issuing authority and source classification.
- Exact URL or repository path; original document identifier/version; Arabic original where applicable.
- Publication date, effective date and retrieval timestamp (Africa/Cairo).
- Relevant page/article/section or quoted factual extract.
- Affected sector, pharmacy module, process, product class and location.
- Verification status, confidence, corroboration and conflicting sources.
- Summary and impact assessment.
- Reviewer/owner, action, decision status and next review date.

## Evidence handling

- Preserve source provenance; do not cite a search snippet when the underlying document is available.
- Separate facts from interpretation and recommendation.
- Do not infer legal force from an authority's news post; classify the underlying instrument.
- Record draft, consultation, amended, withdrawn and superseded documents distinctly.
- Keep source snapshots or hashes when permitted and retain historical versions.
- Do not place patient, employee or confidential supplier data in this register.
- If evidence cannot be accessed or authenticated, mark it unverified and describe the limitation.
- Link material findings to the Egyptian requirements register and affected requirements/decisions.

## Review controls

The task owner confirms scope; the relevant domain specialist validates technical/business impact; qualified Egyptian counsel, regulatory affairs, pharmacist, quality or accountant reviews matters within their remit. The business owner approves business changes. Agent-generated summaries are not professional sign-off.
