# Standards and regulatory references

Use standards as versioned, testable references. A reference is not evidence of certification or compliance.

## ICT and security

- Web/API security: OWASP ASVS 5.0.0; record control IDs and version. OWASP Top 10 2025 is awareness material, not a complete test plan.
- Mobile security: OWASP MASVS and testing resources for storage, authentication, network communication, platform behavior and privacy.
- Secure development: NIST SSDF v1.1 is final; track later drafts as proposals until finalized.
- AI system controls, if applicable: review OWASP AISVS and apply human oversight, output validation and AI risk controls. Do not use AI for autonomous medication or clinical decisions.

## Egypt pharmacy and enterprise requirements

- Egyptian legal and regulatory requirements are tracked in .ai/governance/egypt-requirements-register.md and .ai/governance/egypt-pharmacy-source-register.md.
- EDA regulatory publications, laws, executive regulations, guidance, periodic lists and pharmacovigilance material are primary discovery channels; determine the legal status of each document individually.
- Official Gazette instruments and current amendments are required to establish binding legal text. EDA index pages and government announcements are discovery aids, not substitutes for the original instrument.
- Investigate applicable Egyptian pharmacy practice, establishment licensing, controlled products, product registration/pricing, storage/distribution, medical devices, cosmetics, pharmacovigilance, tax/e-invoice, privacy, consumer and payer requirements.
- Confirm applicability with qualified Egyptian counsel, regulatory affairs, pharmacist, quality and accounting professionals. Do not claim legal compliance from this register.
- International WHO/EMA/FDA or other standards are comparative/scientific references only unless adopted or incorporated by an applicable Egyptian instrument.

## Maintenance

For every external standard or legal reference record title, issuing body, version/instrument number, publication/effective date, source URL, applicable controls, owner, last verification and next review. Recheck before affected design/release decisions. Preserve superseded versions and map changes to tests.
