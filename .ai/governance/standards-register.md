Use community standards as testable references, not as vague claims of compliance:

Web/API security: OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 is the current stable release; record requirement references with the version included. OWASP Top 10 2025 is an awareness resource, not a complete test plan.

Secure development: NIST SSDF v1.1 is final. NIST’s SSDF v1.2 page is still marked draft, so track it as an update to review—not as a finalized requirement.

If the application itself uses AI: review OWASP AISVS 1.0 and add human oversight, output validation, and AI risk controls. Do not use AI for autonomous medication or clinical decisions.
