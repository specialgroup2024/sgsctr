# Open questions

Track each item as: ID, question, impact, owner, status, evidence and decision date.

## Legal entity and operating model
- Q01: Owner reports one registered company, “Special Group”, comprising Pharmacies Sector and Specialized Contracting Sector. Supply/verify exact registered legal name (Arabic and English), legal form, commercial-registration and tax/VAT references, registered activities and effective status from official company records. Impact: statutory books, licensing and tax setup. Owner: business owner/legal adviser. Status: partially answered; official verification pending.
- Q02: Supply the complete pharmacy branch, warehouse, contracting office/site and stock-owning-location list, mapped to legal owner/operator and stock owner. Confirm no site is operated by a separately incorporated company. Impact: branch dimensions, licences, stock custody and entity mapping. Owner: business owner/operations/legal. Status: open.
- Q03: Confirm whether Special Group has a parent/holding company or subsidiaries, or whether it is itself the only relevant legal entity. Provide evidence; do not assume a parent. Owner: business owner/accountant. Status: open.
- Q04: Confirm whether both sectors are legally operated by the same registered person and whether any sector activity is contracted/licensed through another entity. Owner: business owner/legal adviser. Status: open; required before final approval.
- Q05: Which operations/sites are in Phase 1? Owner: business owner. Status: open.
- Q06: Which user roles, approval limits, sector-level access and separation-of-duties rules apply? Owner: business owner/finance. Status: open.

## Decision gate

Provisional planning model: one legal entity LE-01 with two sectors, each having distinct operational controls and management accounting, but sharing one statutory company accounting framework if official records confirm a single legal person. The sector totals must reconcile to company books. No production financial calculation, stock valuation, claim settlement, patient-balance posting or tax configuration until official identity and relevant professional approvals are recorded.
