# Accounting boundaries and rules to confirm

## Entity-level books

- Maintain separate statutory books for every confirmed legal entity.
- Keep contracting and pharmacy books separate regardless of common ownership.
- Attribute transactions to the legal entity that is the actual contracting party, purchaser, stock owner, seller, employer, debtor or creditor, as supported by source documents.
- Branches within one legal entity may use branch/cost-centre dimensions; do not create separate statutory ledgers solely for locations.
- Separately incorporated branches/operations require their own books.
- Confirm chart of accounts, fiscal periods, tax treatment, currency, numbering, opening balances and approval roles with the accountant.
- Define posting, reversal, adjustment, reconciliation and period-close workflows before production accounting is enabled.
- Store monetary values using fixed precision and explicit currency. Preserve an audit trail for posted changes.

## Intercompany

Only record intercompany activity between verified legal entities and where a real transaction exists. Require source documents, counterparty identity, approval, agreed accounting/tax treatment, reciprocal balances and reconciliation. Group reporting must not replace entity-level books.

## Pharmacy inventory costing

The pharmacy business brief specifies weighted-average costing. Before implementation, the accountant and pharmacy lead must approve the costing level and timing, and treatment of receipts, supplier returns, branch transfers, adjustments, negative stock, precision and corrections. Do not silently substitute FIFO or another method.

## Approval restriction

These principles establish boundaries, not a complete chart of accounts or statutory accounting opinion. No production posting, stock valuation, tax calculation, consolidation or financial statement logic is authorized until the relevant professional approves entity-specific policies and worked examples.
