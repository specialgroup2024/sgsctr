# Accounting boundaries and rules to confirm

## Provisional company structure

Owner-provided information identifies one company, “Special Group”, with Pharmacies and Specialized Contracting sectors. Treat this as one provisional legal entity until its exact registered identity and any separate incorporated operations are verified.

If both sectors are the same legal person:
- Maintain one statutory company accounting framework, chart of accounts, fiscal year, tax profile and statutory financial statements.
- Maintain separate sector subledgers/journals or posting dimensions, sector budgets, cost/profitability reports, and sector-level access/approval controls.
- Tag transactions with legal entity and sector; add branch, project, warehouse and other relevant dimensions.
- Reconcile sector subledger totals and allocations to company-level statutory control accounts.
- Do not create intercompany entries or duplicate statutory books between sectors of the same company.

## Pharmacy warehouse and branch accounting

Owner-confirmed arrangement: the main pharmacy warehouse is in Mokattam and distributes stock to the Student Hospital, Qasr El-Aini Hospital and Manial Hospital branches. These locations are administratively connected but must be separately identifiable for accounting and inventory control.

Required system dimensions and controls:
- Distinguish the Pharmacies Sector, Mokattam central warehouse, and each named branch as separate operational locations.
- Maintain location-level on-hand, reserved, available, received, issued, transferred, adjusted, expired and counted quantities as applicable.
- Record each warehouse-to-branch movement with source location, destination location, items, quantities, lot/batch and expiry data where applicable, timestamps, document reference, initiator and approvals.
- Preserve separate branch-level stock valuation and movement reporting, while reconciling to the company/sector inventory control balances.
- Treat transfers between locations of the same legal entity as internal stock movements, not sales or intercompany transactions. Financial posting and transfer-cost treatment require accountant approval.
- Administrative connectivity must not grant unrestricted stock or financial access; enforce role-based permissions and audit every material movement.

Do not infer that these are the only branches, that they are legally licensed/owned by the same entity, or that the Mokattam warehouse is separately incorporated. Verify ownership, operator, licence and stock custody.

If official evidence establishes a second legal entity, it must have its own statutory books and approved data isolation. A branch is a location/cost centre unless separately incorporated.

## Posting governance

Accountant must approve chart of accounts, sector dimensions, branch/warehouse dimensions, allocations, control accounts, fiscal periods, currencies, tax treatment, numbering, opening balances, approval roles, posting/reversal/adjustment rules, reconciliations and period close. Store monetary values using fixed precision and explicit currency; preserve audit trails.

## Intercompany

Only record transactions between distinct verified legal entities where a real transaction exists. Require source documents, counterparty identity, approval, agreed accounting/tax treatment, reciprocal balances and reconciliation.

## Pharmacy inventory costing

The pharmacy business brief specifies weighted-average costing. Before implementation, accountant and pharmacy lead must approve costing level and timing, and treatment of receipts, supplier returns, transfers, adjustments, negative stock, precision and corrections. Do not silently substitute another method.

## Approval restriction

These principles establish boundaries, not a complete chart of accounts or statutory accounting opinion. No production posting, stock valuation, tax calculation, consolidation or financial statement logic is authorized until relevant professionals approve entity-specific policies and worked examples.
