# Accounting rules to confirm

- Use separate accounting books for each legal entity.
- Confirm chart of accounts, fiscal periods, tax treatment, currency,
  numbering, and approval roles with the accountant.
- Define posting, reversal, adjustment, reconciliation, and period-close
  workflows before production accounting is enabled.
- Store monetary values using fixed precision and explicit currency.
- Keep an audit trail for posted financial changes.

# Pharmacy inventory costing

The pharmacy is required by the business brief to use weighted-average
costing. Before implementation, the accountant must approve how the average
is calculated and how receipts, returns, stock transfers, adjustments, and
corrections affect it.

Do not silently substitute FIFO or another costing method.
