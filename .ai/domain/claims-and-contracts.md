# Claims and payer contracts

Do not treat these as the same rule:

- Contract discount from a price.
- Amount or percentage paid by the contracting entity.
- Patient copay or patient share.
- Full patient exemption.
- Claim amount submitted.
- Claim amount approved.
- Remittance received.
- Balance still owed by the payer or patient.

For each agreement, record its effective dates, eligible items or services,
calculation basis, rates, exceptions, approval requirements, and version.

The example "payer covers 80%, patient pays the rest" is not a global
default. Implement it only for an agreement where that rule is approved.

Record claim submission, rejection, adjustment, resubmission, approval,
remittance, and later collection as auditable events. Do not overwrite
posted amounts without an authorized adjustment.

If a contract does not clearly establish the calculation order, add an
open question. Do not infer whether a discount is applied before or after
coverage or copay calculations.
