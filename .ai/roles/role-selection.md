# Per-task dynamic specialist-selection record

Use for substantive work. This records competency perspectives actually selected and performed for one task; it is not a permanent role catalogue or proof that independent people/agents participated. Follow [dynamic specialist selection](../governance/dynamic-role-selection.md).

Identify the dominant ICT field and affected business domain. Select the smallest sufficient set of relevant profiles; add profiles when the task crosses boundaries. Do not force every task into a fixed roster.
