# Workflow
1. Read approved payer agreements, claims domain documents and the central approval register.
2. Identify payer, agreement/version, effective dates, eligible items, exclusions and approved calculation basis.
3. Separate price discount, payer coverage, patient copay/share, full exemption and employee discount.
4. Trace approved basis → patient/payer allocation → claim → approval/rejection → remittance → remaining receivable → collection.
5. Test variable rates, exemption, adjustment, rejection, resubmission, partial payment, later collection and reconciliation using approved examples.
6. Record source-document references, rule version and audit events for each calculation.
7. Escalate unclear contract language and calculation order; keep posting disabled until approved.

# Guardrails
- Do not hard-code an 80/20 or 90/10 example as a universal rule.
- Do not infer the order of discount, coverage and copay calculations.
- Do not create patient balances, claim settlement or financial postings without owner and qualified review.
- This skill does not authorize clinical or pharmacy-practice decisions.
