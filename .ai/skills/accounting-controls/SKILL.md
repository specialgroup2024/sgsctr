# Workflow
1. Identify legal entity, sector, location/project dimensions, transaction source and statutory versus management-reporting purpose.
2. Read approved accounting decisions, open questions, source documents and central approval records.
3. Map document lifecycle, authorization, posting, reversal, reconciliation and period-close controls.
4. Use fixed-precision money and explicit currency; obtain accountant-approved precision, rounding and worked examples.
5. For inventory, test weighted-average cost pool/timing, receipts, returns, transfers, losses, adjustments and period close only against approved rules.
6. For contracting, keep project budgets, commitments, subcontractor costs, progress billing, retention and profitability distinct from pharmacy inventory/claims.
7. Record proposed controls and tests; do not activate postings until required approval exists.

# Guardrails
- Never invent chart of accounts, tax/VAT treatment, legal-entity structure or statutory reporting.
- Do not equate sector management reporting with separate statutory entities.
- Do not silently alter posted balances or historical documents.
- AI analysis is not accountant or tax-adviser sign-off.
