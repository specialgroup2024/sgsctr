# Delivery roadmap

## Phase 0: Discovery and approval

Deliver: confirmed users, business entities, workflows, example calculations,
accounting rules, compliance review, and first-release scope.

Exit: business owners and relevant professionals approve the requirements
that affect money, pharmacy operations, data, and legal obligations.

## Phase 1: Technical foundation

Deliver: repository, CI, API skeleton, database migrations, authentication
approach, audit design, logging, configuration, and test setup.

Exit: clean build, automated checks, migration test, access-control baseline,
and documented local setup.

## Phase 2: Contracting vertical workflow

Deliver one tested workflow from project setup through costs and billing.

Exit: approved business examples pass; permissions and audit events are tested.

## Phase 3: Pharmacy inventory

Deliver product and supplier records, receiving, batch/expiry tracking where
required, stock movements, weighted-average costing, and reconciliation.

Exit: approved inventory and valuation examples reconcile.

## Phase 4: Pharmacy claims and collections

Deliver agreement configuration, claim lifecycle, patient shares,
exemptions, payer remittances, receivables, and collection records.

Exit: approved contract examples pass, including rejection and adjustment cases.

## Phase 5: Pilot and production readiness

Deliver user acceptance, security review, backup/restore test, monitoring,
support runbooks, deployment and rollback plans, and controlled pilot.

Exit: all release gates in definition-of-done.md are met.
